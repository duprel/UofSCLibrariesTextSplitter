To The Men and Women of Good Will in South Carolina:

The Southern Regional Council, Inc. "A Council to attain, through research and action, the ideals and practices of equal opportunity for all peoples of the South."

"The goals summed up in this slogan - higher standards of living, better race relations, political rights for all people, better education and health services, equal justice before the law - these are the aims of the SRC.

"The change from the old approach is this: that SRC will from now on strive to study and solve the problems implicit in those goals as part of the overall problems of the South, which is the region's need to develop a fuller use of its resources, both natural and human.

"That it may have the broad support of the people, the SRC is launching now a campaign to enlist as wide and representative membership as possible. Only from the support of liberal Southerners - men and women of good will, can it muster strength to make its demands for a more prosperous and a more democratic South be heard.

"So will you join with us, and urge your like-minded friends to become members of the Southern Regional Council and take up with us the common task of making the 'New South' a reality? That way - and that way only - lies not only the South's fulfillment, but personal fulfillment, too."

The South Carolina Division of the Southern Regional Council, Inc., will hold its Annual meeting in Columbia, Thursday, April 17, 1947.

Morning Session - 10:30 to 1:00 P.M. at Benedict College

Afternoon Session - 2:00 P.M. to 4:30 P.M. at the First Baptist Church
Sumter and Hampton Streets.

You are urged to attend and to invite any citizens of our state who are interested in such an adventure. "It takes people to make movements."

The program will center about our purpose and our goals.

M A Wright
Marion A. Wright, Chairman
Conway, S.C.
April 12, 1947
