ANNUAL MEETING
South Carolina Division, SRC.
Columbia, S.C.
April 17, 1947

The morning session, consisting principally of business will
be held at Benedict College, from 10:30-12:30 o'clock.

The afternoon session will be held at the First Baptist Church
from 2-4:30 o'clock. Several brief reports will be made, after
which addresses will be made by Dr. Charles L. Hill pastor
of a local Columbia church, and Dr. George Mitchell, Director
of the SRC, Atlanta.

The theme of the meeting will be the Growth and Democratic
Shaping of South Carolina within the Southern Region. Dr. Hill
who will be the first speaker, will discuss this subject from
the philosophical, cultural, and sociological view point. Dr.
Johnson will handle the subject from the point of view of econ-
omics, and action programs.

Both speakers are asked to send to the State newspaper, Columbia,
S.C. and to the office of the Associated Press State , Columbia,
S.C. as soon as possible, a cut if possible, or if not a photo from
which a cut may be made, together with any pertinent facts as to subject
training , work, present connections, and a statement as to the/time
place, and organization before which he will speak in Cola., April 17

If there are further facts desired, address inquiries, either to
Miss Rebecca Reid, 29 E. Calhoun St., Sumter, S.C. or to Mrs E.M. Spear-
man Jefferson Hotel Cola. (MARK, PLEASE HOLD)
