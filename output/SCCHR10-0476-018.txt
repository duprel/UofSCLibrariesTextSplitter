Copy to Havens

Algene Acres
Newberry, S.C.
June 25, 1947

Dr. George Mitchell
Southern Regional Council
Atlanta, Ga.

Dear Dr. Mitchell:

Thank you for a most helpful conference. We appreciate the help, and hospitality of all of your staff. Please express this to them for us.

We wish that you might have participated in our crude beginnings at setting up a program. Dr. Trigg. Dr. Johnson, and Mrs. Havens were most helpful, and we are glad that you could spare them to us.

Yesterday, at the Richland County Comm., a member told me that Mr. Bill Gaines, managing editor of the Greenville News had spoken in a most complimentary way of the position taken by SRC in the Greenville lynching. I am ashamed to say that if there has been anything in the papers, I have missed it, and if there has been any action, I have not heard of it. Will you therefore have someone there send me any facts I should have? I plan to go to Greenville early next month, and would like to know before I go.

We want to invite Ed Ray or Mark Ethridge to come to S.C. early in November for the Forum we hold annually in connection with the State Conference of Social Work. On what basis should they be invited; would they come for expenses? Also, might the request better come from you, or from us? Is there any fund from which the Council can help us with expenses of Speakers? Please let me know about this right away, even if it suits better to send as a pencilled memo.

Am especially sold on the need of leadership training institutes... hope some way can be found to conduct them. Our meeting in Atlanta was the best yet!

With all good wishes, I am

Yours sincerely,
