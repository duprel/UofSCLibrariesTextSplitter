RECOMMENDATIONS
adopted at
The Annual Meeting
Columbia, S. C.
April 17, 1947

RE: LOCAL CHAPTERS

1. That local chapters of the South Carolina Division, Southern
Regional Council be asked to pattern their organization of
committees, etc., after the South Carolina Division as nearly
as is practicable and desirable to the end that greater
coordination of effort may be effected. (See RE: South
Carolina Division Organization.)

2. That Committees of the local chapters keep in touch with
corresponding state division committees, which will try to
serve as a resource and a clearing house, as well as function
on state-wide projects.

3. That local chapters use as far as possible suggestions as to
committee personnel and methods of work since these represent
the basic philosophy of the Southern Regional Council.

4. That all members of local chapters be asked to join the
Southern Regional Council in order to benefit by its south-wide
research and action programs.

5. That all members-at-large of the Southern Regional Council
living in a community, or nearby, be invited to join the local
chapter.

6. That local chapters hold open meetings during the year to which
they invite prospective members living in the county, and in
adjoining counties where there are not yet local chapters.

7. That membership committees of local chapters keep the Executive
Secretary of the South Carolina Division supplied with a
current cultivation list of prospective members to be invited
to state-wide and district meetings, and work out with her
plans for cultivation through the Atlanta office when that is
desired.

MEMBERSHIP AND FINANCE INFORMATION

1. Membership fees should be sent directly to the Southern
Regional Council, 63 Auburn Ave., Atlanta, Ga. Miss Margaret
Price is membership secretary.

2. Membership in the Council automatically confers membership in
the S. C. Division and includes a subscription to the New
South.

3. Membership fee for one person is $2.00 a year. Joint member-
ships of husband and wife are $3.00, if only one subscription
to New South is desired.

4. Of the $2.00 membership fee, $1.00 is retained by the regional
group, and $1.00 is forwarded to the Treasurer of the South
Carolina Division.

5. Local chapters usually charge a nominal membership fee varying
from 50¢ to $1.00 to cover costs of postage, etc.

6. Contributions to the Southern Regional Council may be allocated
for use of the South Carolina Division, and the entire amount
will be forwarded to our Treasurer, or to the General Budget,
and one half will be retained by the regional group, and the
other half returned to our Treasurer.

7. Contributions to the Southern Regional Council are deductible
for income tax purposes.

8. The Finance Committee will secure contributions from those
who wish to extend their participation in this way, as
membership fees alone are not sufficient to carry the cost
of the work, and the possibilities for expansion and develop-
ment at present are great.
